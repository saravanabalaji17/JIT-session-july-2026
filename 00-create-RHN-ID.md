

# Steps to Create a Red Hat Account and RHN ID

### 1. Open the Red Hat Customer Portal

Go to the official Red Hat Customer Portal:

[Red Hat Customer Portal](https://access.redhat.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### 2. Click **Register**

On the top-right corner, click **Register** to create a new Red Hat account.

### 3. Create Your Red Hat Login ID

Enter a unique **Login ID**.

Example:

```text
student.ravi01
```

**Important:** Choose the Login ID carefully because it generally cannot be changed later.

### 4. Create a Password

Create a strong password that meets the requirements displayed by Red Hat during registration.

A strong password should contain a combination of:

* Uppercase letters
* Lowercase letters
* Numbers
* Special characters

### 5. Enter Personal Information

Provide your actual:

* First name
* Last name
* Email address
* Country/Region
* Other required information

Use an email address that you can access because Red Hat will send a verification email.

### 6. Select Account Type

For an individual student, select:

> **Personal**

A **Corporate** account is intended for an organization or business.

### 7. Create the Account

Review the information and click:

> **Create My Account**

Your Red Hat account will then be created.

### 8. Verify Your Email Address

Check your email for the Red Hat verification message.

Open the email and complete the verification process.

### 9. Log in to the Red Hat Customer Portal

Return to the Red Hat Customer Portal and log in using your:

```text
Login ID: student.ravi01
Password: ********
```

### 10. RHN ID / Red Hat Login

The **Red Hat Login ID** is the account identifier you use to access Red Hat services.

It can be used for services such as:

* Red Hat Customer Portal
* Red Hat subscriptions
* Red Hat Developer resources
* Red Hat Learning
* Red Hat certification services
* Red Hat product downloads

### Simple Flow

```text
Register
   ↓
Create Red Hat Login ID
   ↓
Create Password
   ↓
Enter Personal Information
   ↓
Select Personal Account
   ↓
Create Account
   ↓
Verify Email
   ↓
Login to Red Hat Customer Portal
   ↓
Red Hat Account / RHN Login Ready
```

**Note:** The terminology around **RHN (Red Hat Network)** has evolved over time. For current Red Hat services, you will generally see **Red Hat Login**, **Red Hat Customer Portal**, and **Red Hat subscriptions** rather than a separate new RHN ID registration.












# Steps to Create a Red Hat Account and RHN ID

Dear Students,

To enroll in the Red Hat Academy course, you must first create a Red Hat account and share your **RHN ID** with the instructor.

## Step 1: Create a Red Hat Account

1. Open the Red Hat registration page:
   https://developers.redhat.com/register
2. Click **Register**.
3. Fill in the required details:

   * First Name
   * Last Name
   * Email Address
   * Country
   * Create a Password
4. Verify your email address by clicking the verification link sent to your inbox.

## Step 2: Find Your RHN ID

1. Sign in to your Red Hat account.
2. Click your **Profile** (top-right corner).
3. Open **My Profile** or **Account Information**.
4. Locate your **RHN ID** (also called **Red Hat Login ID/Customer ID** depending on the portal).

## Step 3: Share Your Details

Send the following information to your instructor:

* Full Name:
* Email Address:
* RHN ID:

Example:

* Full Name: John Doe
* Email: [john.doe@example.com](mailto:john.doe@example.com)
* RHN ID: jdoe123

After receiving your RHN ID, the instructor will add you to the Red Hat Academy class.

**Note:** Please ensure the email address and RHN ID are correct to avoid enrollment issues.
