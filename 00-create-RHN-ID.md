# Steps to Create a Red Hat Account and RHN ID

Dear Students,

To enroll in the Red Hat Academy course, you must first create a Red Hat account and share your **RHN ID** with the instructor.

## Step 1: Create a Red Hat Account

1. Open the Red Hat registration page:
   https://developers.redhat.com/register
2. Click **Register**.
3. Fill in the required details:

   * First Name
   * Last Name
   * Email Address
   * Country
   * Create a Password
4. Verify your email address by clicking the verification link sent to your inbox.

## Step 2: Find Your RHN ID

1. Sign in to your Red Hat account.
2. Click your **Profile** (top-right corner).
3. Open **My Profile** or **Account Information**.
4. Locate your **RHN ID** (also called **Red Hat Login ID/Customer ID** depending on the portal).

## Step 3: Share Your Details

Send the following information to your instructor:

* Full Name:
* Email Address:
* RHN ID:

Example:

* Full Name: John Doe
* Email: [john.doe@example.com](mailto:john.doe@example.com)
* RHN ID: jdoe123

After receiving your RHN ID, the instructor will add you to the Red Hat Academy class.

**Note:** Please ensure the email address and RHN ID are correct to avoid enrollment issues.
